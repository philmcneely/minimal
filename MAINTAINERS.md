## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer status is earned through sustained, high-quality contribution —
typically several merged image onboardings or non-trivial fixes to the build
pipeline, plus a willingness to review others' PRs. If you've been
contributing regularly and want to take on maintainer responsibilities
(review access, release/merge authority), open an issue or reach out to an
existing maintainer.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Triaging issues
- Upholding the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- Making decisions about project direction, scope, and roadmap
